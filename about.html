01 — BIOGRAPHY
A

ria Velen does not perform songs — she stages memories. Born from the space between a film score and a lullaby, her work treats every track as a single, held breath.

She began recording in borrowed studios at seventeen, layering her voice over field recordings of empty rooms.

Her debut EP arrived in 2021 — sparse, cinematic, and unrelentingly intimate — and quietly gathered a global audience that found her by feeling, not by algorithm.

Today she builds worlds: sound, image, and silence arranged with the discipline of an architect and the patience of a filmmaker.
